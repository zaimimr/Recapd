Researched 2026-10-08. Nothing has been sent. Contact details come from the business's own site where marked "verified", otherwise from public directories. Check each one before you send.

## 1. Partner types, ranked

| # | Type | Why | What they do for us | Risk |
|---|------|-----|---------------------|------|
| 1 | Wedding planners | Many weddings a year, couples follow their vendor advice | Put Recapd in their checklist and on the day-of timeline | Small firms, slow to answer in high season (May-Sep) |
| 2 | Venues (bryllupslokaler, selskapslokaler, party venues) | Repeat volume every weekend, physical space for QR signs | Table signs on every event, mention in booking confirmation | Need a manager to say yes, signage rules |
| 3 | Wedding photographers | Same customer, same moment | Recommend Recapd for guest candids in their contract email | Some see it as competition. Pitch: "guests cover the dance floor and the table, you do the art" |
| 4 | DJs / MCs | They hold the mic all night | Announce the QR code during dinner and before the first dance | Low effort to say yes, but small reach per DJ |
| 5 | Event and party rental (telt, selskapsutstyr) | Ship gear to private parties, confirmations, 50-års | Add a QR sign to every rental package | Least emotional connection to photos, lowest conversion |

Start with 1 and 2. Run 3 and 4 in parallel with a lighter pitch. Use 5 as volume filler.

## 3. Offer structure

Keep it to three parts. No contracts in phase one.

1. **Partner code**
   - Each partner gets a code like `BRUDEPIKENE` and a link `recapd.app/?ref=brudepikene`.
   - Their clients get **one free Pro event** (Pro unlocked for the event's lifetime, granted manually via RevenueCat until self-serve exists).
   - Track signups per code in a simple sheet.
2. **Free Pro for the partner**
   - Partner gets Pro free for 12 months to use at showcase events, open days and their own parties.
3. **Co-branded QR sign**
   - A5 table tent and A4 poster: "Del bildene dine fra kvelden / Share your photos from tonight", event QR, partner logo, Recapd logo.
   - Recapd sends a print-ready PDF per event, or 10 printed signs for venues.
   - Optional later: 20% of the first year of any paid subscription from their code, paid quarterly. Offer only to partners who bring 10+ events.

What we ask in return:
- Planners and photographers: add one line about Recapd in their client info email.
- Venues: put the sign on tables at every private event.
- DJs: one announcement during dinner.
- Rental: one sign in every party package.

## 4. Pitch email, English

**Subject:** Free shared photo album for your couples' guests

Hi [Name],

I'm Zaim, founder of Recapd. It's a private photo album for one event: the host gets a QR code, guests scan it and add their photos and videos straight from the browser. No app to install, no login for guests.

It doesn't replace the photographer. It catches what they can't be everywhere for: the table chats, the dance floor at midnight, the after-party.

I'd like to offer your clients a free Pro event with your own code, plus a co-branded QR sign with your logo for the tables. In return, a one-line mention in your client info is enough.

Would a 15-minute call next week work, or should I just send you a sample sign to look at?

Best,
Zaim Imran
Founder, Recapd
recapd.app | @recapdapp

## 5. Pitch email, norsk bokmål

**Emne:** Gratis felles bildealbum for gjestene på bryllupene deres

Hei [Navn],

Jeg heter Zaim og har laget Recapd. Det er et privat bildealbum for ett arrangement: verten får en QR-kode, gjestene skanner og legger inn bilder og video rett fra nettleseren. Ingen app å laste ned, ingen innlogging for gjestene.

Det erstatter ikke fotografen. Det fanger det fotografen ikke rekker: praten rundt bordet, dansegulvet ved midnatt og nachspielet.

Jeg vil gjerne gi kundene deres et gratis Pro-arrangement med deres egen kode, og et QR-skilt med logoen deres til bordene. Til gjengjeld holder det med én linje om Recapd i informasjonen dere sender kundene.

Passer det med en kort prat på 15 minutter neste uke, eller skal jeg bare sende et eksempel på skiltet?

Vennlig hilsen
Zaim Imran
Gründer, Recapd
recapd.app | @recapdapp
