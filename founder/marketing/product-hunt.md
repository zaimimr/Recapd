## When to launch

- PH days run midnight to midnight Pacific Time. Launch at **00:01 PT**, which is 09:01 Norway time, except Oct 25-31 (08:01) when Europe has changed clocks and the US has not.
- Tue-Thu has the most traffic and the most competition (~30 launches on a Tuesday). A 2026 analysis of ~4,900 launches found weekends need far fewer upvotes for the top 5 (Saturday median ~165 vs Thursday ~235).
- **Recommendation: a Saturday.** A consumer app from a solo maker without a big network has a better shot at a top-5 badge on a quiet day. Halloween timing is a nice hook, so **Saturday Oct 24** if the checklist below is done, otherwise **Saturday Nov 7**.
- Avoid the day of big launches (Apple events, major AI launches). Check the PH upcoming page the week before.
- Plan to be online for the full 24 hours.

Sources: [Databox analysis on PH](https://www.producthunt.com/p/databox/best-day-to-launch-on-product-hunt-here-s-what-4-962-launches-in-2026-actually-show-2), [LaunchList 2026 guide](https://app.getlaunchlist.com/blog/how-to-launch-on-product-hunt-2026), [Screenhance checklist](https://screenhance.com/blog/product-hunt-launch-checklist-2026).

## Pre-launch checklist (start 2-4 weeks out)

- [ ] Use your PH account daily: upvote and leave real comments on other launches. New, inactive accounts get less reach.
- [ ] Follow PH makers in the photo/event space, comment on their launches.
- [ ] Create the "Coming soon" / upcoming page, collect followers.
- [ ] Make the 5 gallery images + optional video. Thumbnail: the app icon, 240x240.
- [ ] Write the first comment, tagline and description in the PH form and save as a draft. Schedule it.
- [ ] Make a list of 30-50 people who will genuinely try it and comment (friends, beta testers, other makers). Ask them to make or warm up PH accounts now.
- [ ] Never ask for upvotes directly or share a direct upvote link. Ask people to "check it out and leave feedback". PH penalises vote rings.
- [ ] Test the full guest flow on a fresh iPhone and a fresh Android in the browser. Fix the landing page FAQ that still says guests must download the app.
- [ ] Prepare a PH-only offer and a landing banner for PH visitors.
- [ ] Draft all launch-day posts (below) in advance.
- [ ] Make sure Sentry alerts and Supabase limits can handle a spike.

## Launch-day checklist

- [ ] 00:01 PT: confirm it's live, post the first comment right away.
- [ ] First hour: message your list personally, one by one, not a mass blast.
- [ ] Reply to every comment within ~15 minutes for the whole day.
- [ ] Post on each channel below, spread over the day so traffic keeps coming.
- [ ] Mid-day (US morning, ~18:00 Norway): second wave of messages to US-based contacts.
- [ ] Watch the funnel: PH visits, joins, new events. Fix anything broken immediately.
- [ ] End of day: thank-you comment on PH, screenshot the ranking.
- [ ] Next day: thank everyone who commented, post results ("what I learned launching solo").
