### Workflow
1. Search the term, open the "Videos" tab, filter by "This month".
2. Open creators whose videos show real parties with guests (not only empty decor).
3. Keep accounts with 5k-50k followers and an average view count of at least 20% of followers on the last 10 videos.
4. Skip accounts where most recent posts are paid ads.
5. Log handle, niche, country, followers, average views, email from bio in a sheet.

### Tools
- **TikTok One** (the Creator Marketplace now lives here): filter creators by country, follower range, category, audience age. Free with a TikTok Ads account. Also handles Spark Ads authorization.
- **Instagram**: native "Partnership ads" and creator search in Meta Business Suite. Check "Open to brand collabs" in creator profiles.
- **Collabstr**: creators list fixed prices, useful for price anchoring (collabstr.com/top-influencers/weddings).
- **Modash / Heepsy / influencers.club**: paid databases, filter Norway + family/lifestyle. Use the free tier to find names, then verify on the platform.
- Manual: look at who comments on bigger party/wedding creators. Smaller creators in the comments are often the right size.

## 3. Creator brief

### Deliverable
- One vertical video, 15-30 seconds, posted on the creator's TikTok or Instagram Reels.
- Filmed at a real party they host or attend (Halloween party, birthday, wedding, julebord, New Year).
- Must show: the QR sign on a table, a guest scanning it with their phone camera, photos appearing in the shared album, and the host scrolling the album the next morning.
- Hook in the first 2 seconds. Ideas:
  - "Stop asking your guests to AirDrop you photos."
  - "Every photo from my party, in one place, and nobody downloaded an app."
  - "The photographer got the posed shots. My guests got the real ones."

### Key message
One QR code, every guest's photos and videos in one private album. Guests join in the browser, no app, no login.

### Do
- Show real guests and real phones. Unpolished is fine.
- Show the album filling up live.
- Say "no app needed" or show the browser.
- Tag @recapdapp and mention recapd.app.
- Use the platform's paid partnership label. In Norway, also write "Reklame" at the start of the caption (Forbrukertilsynet rule). In the US/UK, add #ad.

### Don't
- Don't say it replaces a photographer.
- Don't show anyone who hasn't agreed to be filmed. No children's faces without the parents' clear OK.
- Don't promise features we don't have, or quote prices other than the ones in the app.
- Don't use copyrighted music in the version we run as an ad. Use the TikTok Commercial Music Library.
- Don't show other photo-sharing apps.

### Usage rights
- Organic: Recapd may repost on @recapdapp (TikTok, Instagram) with credit, no time limit.
- Paid: creator gives a Spark Ads authorization code (TikTok) or Partnership ad permission (Instagram) valid for **60 days** from posting.
- Extension beyond 60 days or use in other ad formats: agreed separately, +50% of the fee.
- Creator keeps ownership of the video.

### Payment
- Free Recapd Pro for 12 months.
- **500 NOK** (Norway) or **$50** (US/UK) per approved video, paid within 14 days after it goes live.
- Bonus for a strong performer: second paid video at the same rate.
- Send the video file or draft link for a quick check before posting. One round of small changes max.
