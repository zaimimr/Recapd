# Recapd

**The photo sharing app that lets you live in the moment.**

## The Problem

Every event ends the same way: "Send me your photos!" followed by weeks of waiting, scattered WhatsApp messages, AirDrop requests that never happen, and Google Drive links that expire. Half the photos never get shared. The memories fade.

Photo sharing is broken because it requires effort at the wrong time—when you just want to enjoy the moment or when you've already moved on.

## The Solution

Recapd flips the script. Set up an event with a timeframe, invite your people, then forget the app exists. Live your life. Take photos with whatever camera app you love. When the event ends, we'll remind everyone to share, automatically select the right photos, and create a beautiful timeline everyone can enjoy.

Then the photos disappear. Because memories live in your mind, not in cloud storage you'll never look at again.

## Core Philosophy

### 1. Set It and Forget It
Create an event in 30 seconds. Set the start and end time. Share a code or link. Done. No need to open the app during the event. No interruptions. No friction.

### 2. Live in the Moment
We don't replace your camera. Use the stock camera, VSCO, Halide, ProCamera—whatever you love. Recapd works with your existing photos. We're not trying to change how you capture memories, just how you share them.

### 3. Frictionless Sharing
After the event ends, everyone gets a gentle reminder. Open the app, and your photos from that timeframe are already selected. Review, deselect anything private, tap upload. That's it. No manual selection from thousands of photos.

### 4. Collective Memory
All photos appear in a unified timeline. See the event through everyone's eyes. Discover moments you missed. Remember things you forgot.

### 5. Ephemeral by Design
Photos are deleted 2 weeks after the event ends. This isn't a storage solution—it's a sharing solution. Download what you want to keep. The pressure to "save everything forever" is gone.

## How It Works

```
Create Event → Set Timeframe → Invite People → Enjoy the Moment → Get Reminded → Auto-Select Photos → Upload → Relive Together → Photos Expire
```

### Before the Event
- Create an event with a name, date, and time window
- Share via QR code, link, or invite code
- People join with one tap—no account required

### During the Event
- Close the app
- Live your life
- Take photos however you normally do

### After the Event
- Everyone receives a notification: "Time to share your photos from [Event Name]!"
- Open Recapd, photos from the timeframe are pre-selected
- Quick review: deselect anything you don't want to share
- One tap upload

### Reliving the Moment
- All photos appear in a chronological timeline
- See the event from every perspective
- Download favorites to your camera roll
- 2 weeks later, photos are automatically deleted

## Why This Will Win

### Cross-Platform, Cross-Device
Works on iOS and Android. No more "I can't AirDrop to you" or "My Google Photos won't share to your iCloud." Everyone's included.

### Zero Learning Curve
If you can take a photo, you can use Recapd. No new camera to learn. No complicated settings. No social features to figure out.

### Respects Your Time
- Before: 30 seconds to create an event
- During: 0 seconds (don't open the app)
- After: 60 seconds to review and upload

### Privacy-First
- Photos stay on device until you explicitly upload
- Review everything before sharing
- No AI scanning your entire library
- Data deleted automatically—we don't want your storage costs

### No Lock-In
- Use any camera app
- Download photos anytime
- No subscription required for core features
- Delete your account and everything goes with it

- Concerts, festivals, sporting events
- Dinner parties, game nights, holidays
- Vacations, road trips, reunions

Every single one ends with the same problem: getting everyone's photos in one place. The solutions today are either:

- **Too slow**: "I'll send them later" means never
- **Too permanent**: Cloud storage becomes a graveyard of forgotten folders

Recapd is the only solution that's:
- **Instant**: Pre-selected photos, one-tap upload

- **Ephemeral**: Share the moment, not the storage burden
